ROMs are staged by both Android build and stable in-app release workflows from their original permissively licensed sources.
At runtime the built-in ROMs are copied into app-private storage and merged into the Unified Library cache, while user ROM folders remain intact.
Stable release verification now fails if any of the five built-in ROM assets are missing from the APK.
No commercial ROMs are bundled.
